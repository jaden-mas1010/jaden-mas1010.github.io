DET-001High confidence

Vulnerability Risk Prioritization Pipeline

A Python pipeline that ranks vulnerability scan findings by composite risk — CVSS × asset criticality, boosted by a live CISA KEV exploitation check — so a lower-CVSS finding on a critical, actively-exploited asset correctly outranks a maxed-CVSS finding on a low-value host. Validated against a real Nessus scan of 11 CVE-backed findings, correctly prioritizing two CVEs with 95–99% predicted exploitation likelihood (EPSS) above lower-risk findings. 10 passing unit tests, CI on every push.
